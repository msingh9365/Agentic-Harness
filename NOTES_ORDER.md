## 1. Test 1 - shuffle test: does the model use call order? (April, all 17,402 reports)
**Window and calls.** The window is the first W tokens (W = 512; 4,096 for M2, M4). `m` = the number of calls that lie COMPLETELY inside the window (head length + the cumulated lengths of calls 0..m-1 <= W). The calls m, m+1, ... (the call cut by the window edge and everything after) are never moved. Because the m calls are contiguous and their total length is unchanged by any reordering among them, every variant below keeps exactly the same set of calls in view, in the same token range; the window tokens after the block (the cut call) are identical. Reports with m < 2 cannot change.
**Variants (the shuffle seed is fixed in advance: `numpy.random.default_rng([8100, april_row, variant_id])`, one draw per report and variant, the same edited report is scored by all seeds and models of the same window size):**
- (a) **window-preserving shuffle** (variant_id 1): a uniformly random permutation of the m window calls (no redraw if it is the identity; the share of reports whose order really changed is reported).
- (b) **local shuffle** (id 2): the m window calls cut into consecutive groups of 5 (the last group may be shorter), each group permuted uniformly and independently; groups never cross the window edge (interpretation fixed now: the groups stay inside the window so that the set of calls in view stays the same, as in (a)).
- (c) **reversal** (no randomness): the m window calls in reverse order.
- (d) **reference only, full shuffle** (id 4): a uniformly random permutation of ALL calls of the report (this changes which calls are in view; it shows the confounder). Descriptive; not part of the verdict.
**Reported per model:** clean against (a), (b), (c), (d): pAUC@1e-2, AUC, TPR at 1% (own thresholds), mean absolute change of the logit per report |z_variant - z_clean| (malware and benign separately; also divided by the standard deviation of the clean logits of that class; per seed, then the mean over the seeds), the share of reports with m >= 2 and the share whose order changed, and the mean |change| by the strata m < 2, 2 to 9, >= 10. The difference clean - variant of pAUC@1e-2 with the cluster bootstrap interval and the seeds each way.
**Verdict rule (fixed now; applied to the pAUC@1e-2 difference clean - variant of (a), (b), (c), each model):** *order-sensitive* if the difference is RESOLVED (above) and at least 0.05 (5 points); *bag-of-calls-like* if the whole cluster 95% interval of the difference lies within +-0.02 (2 points) (this is a statement of "no drop larger than 2 points", not of "no change"); otherwise *in between / inconclusive*. The logit changes are descriptive and can modify the plain-English wording but not the category.

## 2. Test 2 - shift / block-boundary test: does the absolute position matter? (April, all 17,402 reports)
**Construction.** The models split the window into 64-token blocks (attention within a block; the paper positional encoding adds the position inside the window). Shifts s are produced by prepending benign calls (inserted directly after the head, before the first call of the report), nested: the targets are t = 0, 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 56, 64 tokens; for t_k the report gets the padding of t_{k-1} and further calls are added right before the original first call until the padding length is as close to t_k as possible but never above it. Call draws: `default_rng([8200, april_row])`; each call = a uniform report of the EVAL pool, then a uniform call of it (pool = the 3,161 benign reports of the group-split validation slice with >= 1 call, the pool of the attacks of NOTES 23, never used for training here). For the last tokens of a target: among 50 draws, a call whose length equals the remainder if one exists, otherwise a random draw with length <= the remainder, otherwise stop (the exact s is lower than the target by the unfilled remainder). The **exact s of every report and target is recorded** (`s_exact` in the saved file). Padding is added to malware and benign reports alike. The tokens of the original report move by s places and the last s tokens of the window fall out; this truncation is part of what a shift does (a confound the control below measures at the level of content).
**Control (content against alignment).** A second set at the single target t = 32: nine independent paddings of the same length (the nested construction is not used; draws from `default_rng([8300, april_row, j])`, j = 0..8, same fill rule). The spread of the logit across these nine equal-length paddings is the amount of change that comes from WHICH calls were added at fixed s. (M1, M3 only.)
**Reported per model:** per s: pAUC@1e-2 (and the cluster interval of its difference to s = 0 with the seeds each way), AUC, **detection of malware at the clean 1% threshold** (the threshold of the unshifted benign scores of the same seed; and the benign false-alarm rate at that threshold), TPR at 1% own threshold. **Spread** of every report = max - min of its logit over the 9 shifts (per seed; then mean and median over the reports of the class, malware and benign separately, mean over seeds; also divided by the standard deviation of the clean logits of the class), next to the spread of the control set (9 equal-length paddings) and the monotone drift (mean logit change against s). M1 against M3: the difference of the mean spreads (malware and benign) and of the range of pAUC over s (max - min), with the cluster bootstrap interval.
**Verdict rule (fixed now):** *flat / alignment-insensitive* if for every s the pAUC@1e-2 differs from s = 0 by at most 0.02 and the detection at the clean threshold by at most 0.02; *alignment- or position-sensitive* if for some s either differs by more than 0.05; otherwise *in between*. Whether a change is "alignment" or only "content / truncation" is read from the shape (a monotone drift in s and a spread not larger than the spread of the control are content/truncation effects; a non-monotone pattern with the spread above the control points to the block structure). This reading is descriptive.
