# Superseded: the ATT+bal numbers of this branch

The ATT+bal (attention pooling with balanced padding) numbers of the length study were produced with the **old training recipe** and the **released tokenizer**. They are **not** the thesis numbers and should not be cited.

The thesis numbers are on the branch `nebula-baseline`, in `RESULTS_FOR_REVIEW.md` (details in `REPRODUCTION_REPORT.md`, sections 5.10 to 5.12): the baseline-v2 recipe, the tokenizer refit on the training slice, a held-out validation slice, 10 seeds, the claim rule of `EVAL_PROTOCOL.md`, and the attacks the models were not trained on.

The rest of this branch (the length, signal and miss analyses, the go/no-go for the two-level model) is unaffected by this note.
