# Loss curves of v2-PE and ATT+bal (logging rerun; no reported result changes)

**What was run.** Two models with the v2 recipe unchanged (lr 2e-3, weight decay 1e-2, batch 96, clip 0.5, dropout 0.3, 90% January slice with the validation hash asserted, BPE-50k, `pe_mode = "paper"`), seeds 0 to 4, **4 epochs** instead of 2 so that the curves show what happens after the chosen stop (epoch 2; nothing was re-selected):
- **v2-PE**: flatten pooling, no padding (the reported single `fit` path).
- **ATT+bal**: attention pooling, balanced padding that replaces the original report (p = 0.5, k ~ U{1..50}, insert or prepend 50/50, TRAIN pool only; built in memory, never written; the reported path).

`baseline/v2pe.py --loss-log` only *reads* what the authors' trainer already stores (the BCE of every training batch, `trainer.train_losses`) and adds evaluation-mode forward passes at the epoch ends; it does not touch training, and it writes no April logits and no weights. Per seed (`<model>/seed<k>.json`): the mean BCE of the last 10 training batches every 10 steps (2,824 steps = 4 x 706); at every epoch end the clean validation loss, the validation pAUC@1e-2, and the loss on a fixed clean subset of 2,000 training reports (numpy seed 11, unedited tokens, evaluation mode). For ATT+bal the per-step training loss is the loss on the **padded training mix** (about half of the reports are edited), so it is not comparable with the clean validation loss; the clean training-subset loss is.

**Check (passed).** For every seed of both models the epoch-2 validation pAUC@1e-2 equals the stored value of the reported run (`results/v2_pe/raw/seed*.json`, `results/att_bal/attbal/seed*.json`): the largest difference is 0.000000 (tolerance 0.002), see `check.json`. The rerun therefore reproduces the reported models exactly up to epoch 2.

**Reading the curves** (`plots/figA_loss_curves.*`, `plots/figB_val_pauc.*`; mean over 5 seeds, values in `summary.json`). For v2-PE the clean validation loss is flat from epoch 1 to epoch 2 (0.099, 0.100) and then rises (0.147, 0.175); its minimum falls at epoch 1 in three seeds and at epoch 2 in two. The loss on the clean training subset falls from 0.074 to 0.047 and then stays near 0.046 to 0.050, so the gap between validation and training-subset loss widens from 0.025 (epoch 1) to 0.053, 0.102 and 0.126: the model overfits after epoch 2, and the validation pAUC@1e-2 peaks at epoch 2 (0.949, 0.967, 0.965, 0.959). The per-batch training loss is noisy for v2-PE (wide min-max band) and flat around 0.05 after epoch 2. For ATT+bal the validation loss does not move monotonically (0.062, 0.078, 0.064, 0.081; minimum at epoch 1 in three seeds, at epoch 3 in two) while the clean training-subset loss keeps falling to 0.021 at epoch 3 (0.023 at epoch 4); the gap grows from 0.018 to 0.043, 0.043 and 0.058, and the validation pAUC@1e-2 is flat from epoch 1 to 3 (0.951, 0.953, 0.954; seed std about 0.009) before it drops to 0.946, so for ATT+bal epoch 2 is not distinguishable from epochs 1 and 3 on validation. The gap for ATT+bal is smaller than for v2-PE at every epoch (0.058 against 0.126 at epoch 4).

**Limits.** 5 seeds (the std between seeds is as large as the differences between neighbouring epochs for the pAUC); the validation slice shares 94.2% of its API sequences with the training slice (REPORT 5.13), so the validation loss measures mostly the loss on seen sequences and the true generalisation gap is larger than the gap shown; the first points of the v2-PE curve start above the top of the axis (the loss at the start of training is above 1).

Code: `baseline/v2pe.py` (`--loss-log`), `baseline/loss_curves.py` (check, summary and plots; `python -m baseline.loss_curves`).
